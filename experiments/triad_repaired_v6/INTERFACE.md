Never retrospectively rescue v5's deicon ballot or v4's A9.

Routes: A pixels to B/C; B pixels to C only; locked votes to Codex; factual report/scores/labeled coaching to all before private logs and C's image; C pixels to next A and next reviewer, never B. C image delivery is separate from its later private log. C12 has no next recipient. Only current A receives the independent private target before voting.

The reviewer schema and synthetic payoffs remain unchanged. Native actor transport failures and completed invalid outputs are distinct. Invalid/refused actor or reviewer observations, unknown readouts and disagreement remain in the denominator and continue through twelve rounds. The first main-round gate checks terminal records/routes/feedback/integrity, not scientific success. Uncertain paid dispatch is never automatically resubmitted; all caps, reservations and old evidence remain enforced.
