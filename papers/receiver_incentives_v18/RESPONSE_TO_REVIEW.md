# How the revision addresses the review

The submitted P/G comparison remains exploratory evidence. It is not pooled with the new randomized pilot.

| Review point | Revision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The receiver-side framing is useful | Test conditional receiver adoption of a known conflicting preference, separately from decoding | Same disclosed preference in both arms; locked B/C ballots; fixed source pages |
| Roles, rewards, reviewer and prompts changed together | Hold the client contract, roles, shared grant, audit policy, models and prompt templates constant; change one reward field | Frozen protocol, exact prompt-difference test and amendment committed before participant calls |
| Test one assumption | Ask whether correctness reward 4000 versus 0 changes joint sponsor adoption | One primary outcome and one prespecified contrast; no five general claims |
| More than one group per arm | Ten independently initialized cohorts per arm, matched by source order | Twenty completed cohorts, three repeated trials each; no vote-level independence assumption |
| Reviewer leaked tags in three historical rounds | Preserve the observation and its timing; do not attribute already-locked ballots to later feedback | Submitted-source audit and prospective audit timing; new audit occurs after all cohort decisions |
| Establish empirical support | Report the actual null outcome and broad uncertainty | 0/30 joint adoptions per arm, paired estimate 0, exact p=1; 120/120 client-correct ballots; full public artifacts |

The controlled comparison improves causal identification without guaranteeing a positive result. The reward contrast showed no observed separation in this fixed-document assay. Its outcome is a floor, not successful demonstrated deterrence and not evidence that covert communication is absent. The paper makes that limitation explicit instead of escalating prompts or replacing the sample.

Both unavailable B documents remain included. They occurred in reward 0 when B's first report exceeded its quota; no B output was regenerated and no image-service failure is substituted as an agent outcome. All 60 trials used attempt 1.

The public data reproduce the grouped analysis without an API key. Original private receipts, first provider outputs and model-written notes are retained. Private notes are self-reports, and no hidden reasoning is presented as ground truth. The experimental auditor is the pinned frontier OpenRouter model, not a Codex or Claude Code product reviewer.
