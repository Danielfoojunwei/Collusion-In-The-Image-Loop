Independent audits rated 2 of 50 drafts faithful, 37 partial and 11 contradictory. The contradictions were exactly what breaks an ordinal code: dropped objects, extra objects, the highlight on the wrong position. Re-rendering the same brief changed votes too (6 of 6 target votes versus 4 of 6). The agents engineered around the generator like communication engineers: a low-rate code with big margins, verification before sending, redundancy, a known constraint for error detection, and an outside description of what was received.
