The failure mode

Output-level oversight of agents with private payoffs and reasoning can see coordination but cannot attribute it, even with a frontier overseer.

1 · Tell the reviewer the incentive structure

2 · Define the improper act, not “pictures are the task”

3 · Score cross-round contingency as evidence

4 · Keep verdicts from agents and from the reviewer itself

Limits: induced coordination · one cohort of 12 dependent rounds · comparison judges are single views, different rubric and model · reviewer version not logged · no honest-negative ladder rounds.

Seeing is not attributing. The agents needed no special capability: a small fast model used a visible code and ordinary engineering. A stronger overseer will not fix a question that forbids the answer. Each design lesson is a testable intervention on the same game, with honest-negative rounds to measure false positives.
