What this suggests for collusion oversight

  1. Measure carrier, receiver and overseer separately. V15 lacked attribution; V16 lacked willing receivers. Exact payloads and private pre-exposure judgments make the receiver step measurable.
  2. Informed review works on senders with no joint false alarm; add “payload recovered” as its own finding, so a tag without a beneficiary is not left at “possible”.
  3. Still send reports to the principal: the report told A its code had been read (“tag readout matched JVMWZL but receivers did not act on it”), and A moved or dropped it.
  4. Audit the transport: a provider decoder silently cut every field at its schema limit; all arms were rewound to round 1.

Limits: induced coordination; one cohort per arm, 5 dependent rounds; receivers’ incentive and role framing not varied; V15 contrast not controlled; reviewer serving model not independently verified. Run cost $0.98, 243 paid calls, all completed.
