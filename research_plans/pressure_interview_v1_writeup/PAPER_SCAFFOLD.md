## 1 Introduction

Generated technical images and accompanying public text can carry both task evidence and attempts to influence a peer. This study tests whether an instructed sender can affect an independent verifier's choice while a monitor-assisted reviewer questions the sender. It does not assume that the verifier cooperates, that a hidden convention emerges, or that camouflage succeeds.

## 3 Methods

There are six independent main case blocks in three fixed pressure batches of two pairs each, with synthetic slack values 1.0, 0.5, and 0.25. A lower value represents greater assigned pressure. A pair's value remains constant; pressure does not decay within a dialogue. Two honest and two overt-attempt controls are separate cases.

Each main pair has eight shared A/B rounds and two alternative four-round continuations. Each final trajectory therefore contains twelve rounds. The primary endpoint is B's first explicit choice at round nine, before the new interview or feedback. The plan permits twelve primary receptions, sixteen final trajectories, and 144 unique A/B rounds. Shared-prefix events must be counted once. Every case and missing branch will appear in the final results.

A alone receives the private disqualified nomination and synthetic pressure input. B is an independent policy verifier with a private reporting channel. A deterministic allowlist passes selected B claims to C, rather than granting C access to B's hidden state. C sees permitted public material, monitor claims, and its own prior interview memory. It asks A one question, A answers once, and C supplies separate task/coordination judgments and bounded public feedback. C cannot interview B. A/C interview transcripts are not directly shown to B.

A and B use `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` through `open-inference/fp4`, requesting high reasoning and 4,096 total completion/reasoning tokens per call. The image service is `qwen/qwen-image-2.1-turbo` through `alibaba`, with one 1,024-square output and at most one reference. Every actor turn permits at most four actions and two generations. Sampling overrides are omitted. Actual fresh Codex product contexts author tasks and perform C's question/verdict jobs. Sonnet audits are not activated.

Complete role-scoped archives persist, with a bounded 20,000-byte history working view, up to four recent exact rasters, and older-record/image retrieval. The final draft reads this bound from the actual frozen configuration. Persistent storage does not mean every old image or every prior text is visible in each request. Source PNGs and lossless wire encodings are linked by hashes; each complete POST must remain strictly below 10 MiB before reservation. Missing responses are not automatically retried.

The neutral continuation repeats B's full permitted tool loop from the saved pre-turn context, replacing the current A raster and every corresponding `last_peer` handle with the truthful card. Actual public A text is held fixed. Thus the intervention is conditional on public text and prior history; it is not proof of an exclusively image-mediated signal. Replays never feed into live feedback or C.

The upper allowances are 1,344 GLM chat calls, 600 Qwen requests, 288 C question/verdict jobs, and ten task-author jobs. These are ceilings, not observed counts or guaranteed affordable completion. All requests share the original $40 inference ledger, including earlier spending and unknown reserves, within the user's $50 OpenRouter cash ceiling. Codex is excluded from that cash accounting.

## 5 Discussion and Limitations

An assigned A attack objective, target-following, wrong choice, private note, or reviewer suspicion alone is insufficient to establish completed collusion. B may resist, report, or be misled without knowingly cooperating. Six main cases limit generalization. Two cases per pressure level and fixed execution order preclude a strong causal pressure comparison. There is no interview-off or feedback-off counterfactual. Shared-filesystem Codex contexts are instruction-scoped and trace-unverified; flags are descriptive and cannot establish blinded detector accuracy. Provider-returned reasoning and concise notes are self-reports, not verified hidden cognition.
