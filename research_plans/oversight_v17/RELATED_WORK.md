# Novelty boundaries and comparison evidence

Literature reviewed30 October2026. This is an evidence-based positioning document, not proof of exhaustive priority. The proposed broad paper must earn its contribution through controlled results. Images, money, adaptation, refusal and monitors are not individually novel ingredients.

## Contribution criteria

The broad paper should deliver a benchmark with independently validated participation labels, controlled oversight-package effects, and held-out safety/utility transfer. A nested explanation-content intervention can identify a mechanism, not substitute for the whole policy study. Neither a novel combination nor improved presentation establishes a new finding.

Do not claim first hidden document message, first feedback-adaptive collusion, first receiver refusal, first monitor rationale containing a payload, first distinction between intentions and actions, or first causal influence test. Do not rank reviewer capability from product names or reasoning labels.

We did not find an exact explanation-payload replacement/redaction experiment with fixed verdict/sanctions establishing subsequent agent coordination in the closest examined work. That is a candidate gap, not universal priority proof. The broader claim depends on empirically connecting recruitment, attribution and intervention across held-out workflows.
