## Research question

Which combinations of receiver incentives, monitor evidence and intervention policy reduce harmful coordination while preserving useful collaboration and avoiding false participant sanctions?

V15 and the completed V16 report are exploratory motivation. V16 is no longer the paused pre-amendment004 snapshot: subsequent amendments and the completed report are present in the baseline. Do not execute old run instructions or pool historical observations into new confirmatory estimates. The old AGENTS.md pause narrative and the newer CLAUDE.md differ; neither establishes this new proposal's launch readiness.

## Four connected studies

1. Receiver recruitment: correctness reward on/off crossed with shared grant on/off, roles and communication fixed.
2. Monitor attribution: fixed trajectories, four evidence views, matched model-family and effort comparisons.
3. Live oversight: sanctions crossed with feedback disclosure, plus a shadow baseline and a figure-removal comparison.
4. Transfer: frozen policies on held-out tasks, a second actor family and text-only versus document carriers.

The broad contribution is a causal account of oversight effectiveness across the recruitment-to-harm process. Monitor explanation leakage is a nested mechanism, not the entire paper. Each experiment isolates a smaller question.
